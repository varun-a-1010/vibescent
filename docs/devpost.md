**Tagline:** Your outfit already knows what you should smell like.

## Inspiration

Fragrance shopping starts with notes and marketing copy, but people think in terms of how they want
to come across. An outfit and an occasion already say a lot about taste, and taste is what Qloo
understands.

## What it does

Describe a look or add a photo. VibeScent proposes cultural touchstones, confirms them on Qloo, shows
you the aesthetic cues it found so you can remove any that miss, and returns real fragrance samples
with reasons for each. Your budget and limits always win over taste signals.

## Why Qloo is essential

Qloo is the step between a look and a scent: it turns "leather jacket at a rooftop bar" into real
cultural entities and aesthetic tags. Without it, the brief would be a language model's guess.

## How we built it

Gemini handles vision and writes a scent brief in a controlled vocabulary. Qloo supplies confirmed
entities and tags. A live fragrance catalog filters and ranks. Gemini 4 Argon leads, with a second
Vertex project and a chain of Flash models behind it. TypeScript, Fastify and React.

## Limits

Only about 50 fragrances carry vibe tags so far, and wearing profiles are estimates. Qloo shows
cultural association, not what will suit you.
